# Style

- Number results globally (Theorem 1 ... Proposition 53), not by section. Section-based numbering
  collides with section numbers and breaks on renumbering.
- State scope with each result: what it assumes, what it does not cover.
- Say "conditional on" wherever a result rests on a cited input, and name the input.
- Identifiers and long paths in `\texttt{}`; check the build for overfull boxes and fix them in the
  source, not with spacing hacks.
