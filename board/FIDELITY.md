# Statement fidelity

Maintained by the auditor. One row per result in the paper. Build it from the first formalized claim,
not at the end: it is the artefact that makes "machine-checked" mean something, and it is where prose
drifting from the formal statement shows up.

Grades for an instance of a hypothesis structure:
- **Nontrivial:** a genuine model of every field.
- **Degenerate:** a model, but one that is no evidence any field is non-trivially satisfiable.
- **Vacuous:** satisfies the fields only by making their own premise false.

Neither degenerate nor vacuous instances are evidence that the fields hold non-trivially.
