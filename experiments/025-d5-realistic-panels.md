## Question

This is D5's item-13 completion stage (PM note `2026-10-06-exp-d5-realistic-panels.md`).
- **Item 13's done condition:** "a comparison against the four competitors in which the lab's test has controlled
  false alarms and reported power on realistic designs, including misspecified ones".
- Experiments 019 and 022–024 measured single names. This stage measures **panels** of correlated names.

**Headline question.** Does usable test v1 with e-BH keep false discoveries controlled on realistic nulls, and what
fraction of bubbly names does it find? The answers are set against the competitors' rates on the same paths.

**Jobs, kept apart:**
- **Calibration under the guarantee:** GBM-with-drift nulls, which are inside claim 055's class.
- **Performance outside the guarantee:** Merton jumps, regime-switching volatility and Heston with leverage. Each is a
  true martingale whose law is outside claim 055's class.

No result exists when this Design is committed. Nothing will be retuned after seeing results.

## Design

**Tests.**
- **Primary: usable test v1** (`experiments/021-d6-controls.md` Deviation 1).
  - The score is claim 055's E^drift on experiment 022's 18-candidate catalogue p ∈ {0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.25, 1.5, 2} ×
    κ ∈ {0.20, 0.30, 0.45}, with null p ≤ 1.
  - Split ⌊N/2⌋; prices normalized to S/S0; time in years at 252 trading days.
  - Base e-BH ([wang2020false]; claim 053) across the m names of each panel at q ∈ {0.05, 0.10}, with the original m.
    Failures are kept as zero e-values.
  - **Secondary, labelled:** claim 054's raw score under the same e-BH. It has no guarantee off the catalogue.
- **Competitors,** with experiment 015's implementations and tunings, on the same S/S0 paths:
  - **SADF and GSADF** ([phillips2012multiple]):
    - minimum window ⌊(0.01 + 1.8/√N)N⌋;
    - per-name p-values (1 + #{null ≥ stat})/(R + 1) from R = 2,000 simulations of [phillips2012multiple] (3) at each
      N, with SeedSequence(20261125);
    - Benjamini-Hochberg ([benjamini1995controlling]) across the panel at q ∈ {0.05, 0.10}.
    - BH assumes independence or positive dependence. Under arbitrary dependence, Benjamini-Yekutieli
      ([benjamini2001control]) is also reported.
  - **JKP Methods 2 and 1** (015's wrapper): per-name verdicts, with no p-values. A name is a discovery when the
    verdict is "bubble". Indeterminate shares are reported.
  - **The panel test** ([liu2022panel]; 015's Deviation 1 reading, on S/S0 − 1): one statistic per panel, so it gives
    a panel-level verdict at the one-sided 1.64 (5%) and 1.28 (10%) critical values, not name discoveries. Its
    p-value is 1 − Φ(t), reported per panel.
  - **OPY:** unsupported, because it needs intraday bars and these panels are daily.

**Panels.**
- **Size:** m ∈ {20, 100} names. 100 panels per cell for m = 20 (2,000 names) and 40 panels per cell for m = 100
  (4,000 names).
- **Horizon:** daily sampling, T ∈ {5, 10} years (N = 1,260 and 2,520 increments). Prices start at 1.
- **Composition:** bubbly fraction f ∈ {0, 0.10, 0.30}. The first round(f·m) names of a panel are bubbly and the rest
  are null.
- **Bubbly names:**
  - CEV dS = κ S^p dW with p ∈ {1.5, 2}, the same p for all bubbly names of a panel, and drift zero. These are strict
    local martingales.
  - κ_i ~ U[0.20, 0.45] independently, so off the catalogue grid almost surely.
  - Simulated exactly with experiment 022's sampler (`experiments/022/cev.py`).
  - **Limitation:** the exact sampler has no factor loading, so bubbly names are independent of the market factor.
    Cross-name dependence comes from the null names.
- **Null names (all true martingales under Q),** with one market factor per panel.
  - Each null name's diffusive driver is W_i = ρ W_m + √(1 − ρ²) B_i, with ρ = 0.5 (pairwise correlation 0.25) and
    independent B_i.
  - Physical proportional drift b_i ~ U[−0.10, 0.20] for every null type. Under P = Q this drift is not a pricing
    statement: the pricing label is "true martingale" under the driftless Q law of the same type, as in experiments
    022–023.
  - Null types, one per cell:
    1. **GBM with drift.** Scale κ_i ~ U[0.15, 0.60]; log-increments (b_i − κ_i²/2)Δ + κ_i ΔW_i, exact. **Inside**
       claim 055's class, so covered.
    2. **Merton jumps (AX-59 law).**
       - Diffusion σ_i ~ U[0.15, 0.45], intensity λ_i ~ U[0.5, 1.0] per year (a jump every one to two years), and log
         jump N(μ_J, η_J²) with μ_J = −0.05 and η_J = 0.10.
       - Log drift a = b_i − λ_i k − σ_i²/2, with k = exp(μ_J + η_J²/2) − 1, so the proportional drift is b_i.
       - Exact: per day, a Poisson count, then a Gaussian given the count. The diffusion part loads on the factor.
       - **Outside** claim 055's class.
    3. **Two-regime volatility switch.**
       - Volatility κ ∈ {0.15, 0.45}, switching as a continuous-time Markov chain with rate 1 per year each way.
       - The regime is held constant within a day and switches between days with probability 1 − e^(−1/252). The
         initial regime is uniform.
       - Given the regime path the increments are exact GBM ones, so the price is a martingale under the driftless
         law.
       - **Outside** claim 055's class.
    4. **Heston with leverage.** dS/S = b_i dt + √v dW_i and dv = κ_v(θ − v)dt + ξ√v dZ_i, with d⟨W_i, Z_i⟩ = −0.7 dt,
       κ_v = 2, θ = 0.09, ξ = 0.5 (Feller 2κ_vθ = 0.36 > ξ² = 0.25), and v_0 = θ.
       - Simulated with 10 sub-steps per day: full-truncation Euler for v, and the exact conditional log-normal price
         step given v on each sub-step. Given the simulated variance path, the discounted driftless price is exactly a
         martingale.
       - The variance scheme only approximates Heston's law; the label is the simulated law's.
       - **Outside** claim 055's class.
       - No Heston (1993) text is registered, so the dynamics are stated here in full.
- **Cells:** 4 null types × 5 compositions (f = 0; f = 0.10 with p = 1.5 and with p = 2; f = 0.30 with p = 1.5 and
  with p = 2) × m ∈ {20, 100} × T ∈ {5, 10}, giving 80 cells. Every registered cell is reported, and a missing cell is
  explained.

**Labels.**
- A name's truth is "bubbly" (CEV p > 1, a strict local martingale) or "null" (a true martingale).
- For the lab's test, a null name is **covered** only in the GBM cells and **uncovered** in the jump, regime and
  Heston cells. False discoveries on uncovered nulls are out-of-class measurements, not size.
- The competitors' own null hypotheses differ (a unit root for PSY, a local-time volatility estimate for JKP). For
  them every null name is a true martingale, and the comparison counts flags against these labels.

**Metrics, per cell and per test** (Monte Carlo SE across panels; binomial SE for per-name rates).
- **Realized FDP** per panel, with mean FDR and its SE. "Panels with FDP > q" is reported as a share.
- **Discoveries:** mean true discoveries and false discoveries per panel; per-name power, the share of bubbly names
  discovered.
- **All-null panels (f = 0):** the family-wise false-alarm rate, the share of panels with at least one discovery.
- **Per-name flag rates** at α = 0.05 for the single-name versions: E^drift > 20, the raw score > 20, SADF/GSADF
  p < 0.05, and JKP "bubble". These link to the single-name experiments.
- **The panel test:** the panel-level rejection rate.
- **Failures:** status counts for the lab's scores (numerical failure, outside domain), JKP failed and indeterminate
  shares, and any SADF/GSADF failure.

**Seeds and paths.**
- `SeedSequence(20261025)`, with one child per cell in the order null type (1–4), composition (as listed), m, T.
- Ten children per cell, one per batch. Each batch holds a tenth of the cell's panels: 10 panels for m = 20, 4 for
  m = 100.
- All tests see the same paths. No extra paths, seed changes or stopping after inspecting results.

**Implementation and checks.**
- **Vectorized score.** The lab's score is computed by experiment 022's vectorized implementation (`cev.score`, the
  claim-055 column), with 023's large-argument Bessel fix.
- **Validation.**
  - It is checked against the total adapter `checks/d13-drift-total/total.py` on the first three names of every cell
    (240 names): raw and enlarged log E, fit and status.
  - Every name with |log E + log α| < 10⁻⁶ for α ∈ {0.05, 0.10} is also checked.
  - A decision mismatch is recorded and the adapter's value is reported beside it.
- **Failures** are kept as zero e-values and never replaced by the raw score.
- **Commit order.** Code is committed before any result, and every change is recorded under Deviations before
  resuming.
- **Running.** Run inside `.worktrees/analyst` with the single-thread BLAS prefix, on cores − 3 workers.
- **Outputs:** `experiments/025/results.json`, `statuses.json`, `summary.csv`.

**Limits stated with every table.**
- Exact observations, with no rounding or microstructure noise.
- Bubbly names are CEV only and independent of the factor.
- Parameter ranges are illustrative, not calibrated to an asset class.
- e-BH controls FDR under arbitrary dependence; BH needs independence or PRDS, which these factor panels do not
  guarantee.
- This is a simulation, not real data.
