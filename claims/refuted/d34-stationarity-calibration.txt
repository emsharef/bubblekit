Refuted target: stationarity, identical score marginals, a fixed window length,
or removing overlap is enough to give a finite-sample level-alpha guarantee for
a threshold estimated from earlier calm windows.

For any integer cycle length m > 19, let U be uniform on {0,...,m-1} and
S_t=(U+t) mod m for all integer t. This is strictly stationary because shifting
time permutes the uniform phase. With 19 calibration scores S_0,...,S_18 and
alpha=1/20, even the conservative conformal cutoff is their maximum. The event
S_19 > max(S_0,...,S_18) has probability (m-19)/m, tending to one. At m=1000
the exact probability is 981/1000. Sampling every second time gives 962/1000
and can be represented as terminal-value scores of disjoint length-two windows.
This is an observation-law obstruction, not a GSADF process construction or a
proof that no useful guarantee exists under quantified dependence conditions.

The existing interpolated, inclusive rule has separate problems even with
exchangeability: constant scores trigger with probability one; uniformly
permuting 20 distinct scores gives a 10% rejection rate at its interpolated
lower 5% cutoff using 19 calibration scores. Neither example estimates the
error rate in experiments 040/045.
