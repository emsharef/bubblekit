D35 input refuted: bounding the positive baseline variance away from zero
makes the conditional density profile finite when beta ranges over beta<=0.

The mechanism is the narrow-scale likelihood singularity already recorded
in D15's unrestricted-null-supremum refutation (related prior art:
[snoussi2001mixtures]). This is an explicit application counterexample,
not a new general singularity theorem or a claim of impossibility for D35.

Use any continuous everywhere-positive density f with 0<f(0)<infinity,
including the candidate standardized Student-t. Set h_t=1 for every t,
s=S_0, x_(t-1)=log(S_(t-1)/s), v_t=exp(2 beta x_(t-1)), and
p_t(r)=f((r-mu)/sqrt(v_t))/sqrt(v_t). The nuisance location mu is common
to both returns and is fitted over a domain containing the second return.
For R_1=a>0 and R_2=b, take mu=b. Then

    p_1(a) p_2(b) = f(a-b) f(0) exp(-beta a) -> infinity

as beta -> -infinity, although the baseline h is identically one. This
occurs on an open set of two-return records when mu ranges over an interval,
not merely at a prespecified zero-probability return. Each finite candidate
is a proper conditional density; it is the supremum that is infinite.

The baseline recursion in checks/d35-repair-input/README.md admits h_t=1
with omega_h=1 and a_h=c_h=b_h=0, h_init=1. The example also permits a
fixed smaller baseline by the corresponding rescaling.

Strongest corrected route at this stage: keep the unrestricted null and
define its profiled evidence as zero whenever that prefix profile is
infinite, subject to measurability and the actual-law assumptions in the
later application. Do not erase earlier evidence. No claim that all later
prefixes remain infinite is established. Restricting beta or flooring the
total variance could change this obstruction, but changes the model; neither
is silently adopted. A local or finite-grid maximum is a lower bound on the
profile and cannot replace the dominating denominator of a valid e-process.

The deterministic fixtures in checks/d35-repair-input/check.py verify the
displayed log likelihood's growth; they are not a fitted score or a proof
that the requested repair lacks useful power.
