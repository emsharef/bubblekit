Refuted target: declaring finite drift/scale ranges from a disjoint earlier
window automatically preserves claim 057's conditional calibration, or a
small declaration failure probability controls the unbounded raw score mean.

The missing premise is coverage of the actual later conditional law after
conditioning on the entire earlier record. Constant parameters and independent
increments supply the conditional likelihood, not probability-one membership
in a range estimated from a finite earlier sample. The positive Gaussian
finite-sample densities have identical null sets: probability-one coverage of
every positive integer scale would force the scale upper bound to be infinite
almost surely under every scale.

A concrete price-kernel counterexample has actual driftless GBM scale one,
finite catalogue {(p=1,κ=1/2),(p=2,κ=1)}, one training and one evaluation
increment of duration one. On an independent earlier event of any positive
probability at most δ, declare B={0}, C={1/2}; otherwise declare B={0}, C={1}.
Large training endpoints select p=2 with positive probability. There its
evaluation density has a positive y^(-4) tail (AX-54). With y=x exp(r), the
actual expected raw-score integrand is asymptotic to a positive constant times
exp(3r²/2−3r), so the raw score has infinite mean despite small coverage failure.
The construction and diagnostic checks are in proposed claim 058 and checks/058/.

Corrected target: conditional calibration on genuinely covered earlier records,
or unconditional size and clipped-score guarantees with a proved declaration
failure budget. A scale change requires a budget for the later law, not merely
coverage of the earlier parameter. This does not refute every training-adaptive
method with separately verified conditional-law and coverage premises.
